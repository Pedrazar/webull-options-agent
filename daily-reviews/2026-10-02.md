## Bug found and fixed: a real close silently dropped from the log

Checked the broker's own order record directly (same diligence as every other incident this project has hit): `status: FILLED, filled_price: 0.50, filled_time_at: 2026-10-02T14:13:22Z` — 13 minutes after the order was placed at 14:00:31Z. The 2026-09-21 fix (only log success on confirmed FILLED) worked exactly as intended in NOT fabricating a sale — but it also didn't do anything to recover the real outcome once it eventually resolved, so a genuine $250 gain just vanished from the books. The actual trading was never wrong (the next tick correctly saw the position gone and sold the next put) — only the accounting was.

**Fixed**: unresolved closes (genuinely unknown status, not a definite cancel/reject) are now persisted in `wheel-state.json` and re-checked at the start of every tick until they resolve, at which point the real event gets logged with the real fill price. Full detail in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Known asymmetry, not fixed**: the same gap exists on the open side (a slow-filling `SELL_TO_OPEN` could similarly miss its `put_sold` log line) — not fixed because `reconcile()` already reads the real credit from the broker's own position data regardless, so the only cost there is a missing audit-trail line, not a wrong `cumulativePremium`.
